# Brainrot Identity Gate

## Product identity

Brainror Game is not a generic alien-creature battler. The playable roster must read immediately as **original absurdist brainrot hybrids**: familiar animal/creature anatomy fused with an ordinary food, appliance, vehicle or household object, with rhythmic pseudo-Italian naming and an intentionally over-serious presentation of a ridiculous concept.

The game may use the broad visual/comedic language associated with Italian-style internet brainrot, but every character must remain original project IP. Do not reproduce, trace, rename or lightly remix a recognizable existing viral character.

## Character formula

Every playable family must pass all of these checks:

1. **Two-part joke** — the creature half and mundane-object/food half are both readable without the name.
2. **96 px silhouette** — the hybrid still reads on a phone-sized board slot.
3. **Mundane anchor** — scooter, espresso cup, focaccia, gelato cone, colander, router, vending machine, umbrella, mop, fan, sock, microwave, etc. The prop cannot be replaced by generic sci-fi glow.
4. **Face and attitude** — clear eyes/mouth/posture; characters should feel like personalities, not decorative blobs.
5. **Escalating merge tiers** — T2 adds a second absurd prop/material beat; T3 pushes the joke into grandiose excess while preserving the family silhouette.
6. **Name cadence** — short, memorable pseudo-Italian rhythm built from generic words and original combinations.
7. **IP distance** — no protected character silhouette, signature phrase, brand, logo, celebrity likeness or recognizable viral-character recreation.

## Rejection criteria

Reject or redesign a playable character if:

- it reads primarily as an alien, slime or space mascot;
- neon glow is doing more identity work than the mundane hybrid prop;
- the object half disappears at gameplay size;
- its three merge tiers look like color swaps instead of escalating absurdity;
- the name could be confused with a known protected meme character;
- the silhouette depends on a logo or branded product shape.

## Starter-family target

The first four families establish the promise in the first minute:

- **Pinguino** — penguin + generic retro motor scooter + espresso machinery.
- **Toastodilo** — crocodile + focaccia/panzerotto + countertop oven/toaster.
- **Lampalotl** — axolotl + gelato/granita cone and dessert-cart language.
- **Dishnail** — snail + colander + spaghetti/ravioli artillery.

A fresh player should understand the game's absurd brainrot premise from these four silhouettes before opening any collection screen.

## Later-family target

Later families keep their mechanical IDs for save compatibility but should follow the same art gate: tiramisu moth, modem raptor, vending-machine iguana, umbrella llama, mop opossum, haunted fan, laundry octopus and microwave whale. Internal IDs are implementation details; public names and art carry the fantasy.
