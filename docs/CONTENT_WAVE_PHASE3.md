# Launch Candidate Content Wave — Families 8–12 + Endless Bosses

This sheet defines the authored identity for the Phase 3 roster expansion. All concepts are project-original combinations of everyday objects/materials and animals/ghost shapes. None intentionally reproduces a known meme character, celebrity, streamer, mascot, franchise design or brand product.

## Umbrellama — unlock Chapter 13

**Visual joke:** a compact llama carrying its own storm umbrella canopy as part of its silhouette.

- Silhouette primitive: rounded llama body + very wide triangular/domed umbrella top.
- Dominant prop/material: glossy rain canopy, hooked handle, soft fleece body.
- Personality: cheerful weatherproof mascot; eyes remain visible below the canopy at small size.
- Gameplay role: defensive support; `Storm Canopy` adds a second, lighter fortress-damage reduction multiplier.
- Evolution read: Drizzle uses a simple canopy; Monsoon gains a bright rim/crest; Tempest gains a large crown/energy cap.
- Mutation hooks: Charged can crawl along umbrella ribs; Prismatic wings sit behind the canopy; Crowned halo floats above the umbrella rather than over the face.
- Animation opportunities: canopy squash on fire, raindrop recoil, umbrella snap-open on synergy tier change.
- IP-distance: generic llama + umbrella/weather language only; no copied mascot costume, logo or known meme silhouette.

## Mopossum — unlock Chapter 16

**Visual joke:** an opossum whose tail and cleaning tool read as a scavenger mop rig.

- Silhouette primitive: low oval body + long asymmetric mop/tool diagonal.
- Dominant prop/material: wet microfiber/mop head against neutral fur/chrome evolution accents.
- Personality: smug cleanup scavenger, more industrious than cute.
- Gameplay role: economy support; `Salvage Sweep` increases combat coin bounty without changing real-money/ad logic.
- Evolution read: Bucket → larger Scrub rig → reflective Chrome cleaning crown/crest.
- Mutation hooks: mutation coils can wrap the mop shaft; wings remain clear behind the low body.
- Animation opportunities: mop swipe on attack, tail drag follow-through, coin-spark sweep when bounty synergy activates.
- IP-distance: generic opossum + janitorial object combination, no brand packaging or recognizable commercial mop design.

## Fanthom — unlock Chapter 18

**Visual joke:** a friendly haunted floor fan whose rotating blades are its ghost heart.

- Silhouette primitive: tall ghost shell around one large circular fan disc.
- Dominant prop/material: translucent cool shell + high-contrast central rotor.
- Personality: calm floating ghost with an obviously mechanical center.
- Gameplay role: cadence support; `Ghost Fan` multiplies existing squad attack interval reduction at a deliberately smaller magnitude than Pinguino.
- Evolution read: Breeze has a clean rotor; Cyclone gains an outer airflow crest; Poltergust gains a crown/halo and larger shell.
- Mutation hooks: Prismatic wings must sit outside the fan disc; Charged energy traces blade tips.
- Animation opportunities: blade spin ramps before projectile, shell wobble on impact, fast reverse-spin on defeat/merge.
- IP-distance: original ghost + generic ventilation fan mashup; name and silhouette do not target a protected ghost appliance/franchise.

## Socktopus — unlock Chapter 21

**Visual joke:** a laundry octopus whose tentacle tips terminate in oversized sock-like soft forms.

- Silhouette primitive: round head + four long readable lower arms with big terminal shapes.
- Dominant prop/material: soft knit/laundry color blocking.
- Personality: mischievous multi-tasker with expressive face above the arm mass.
- Gameplay role: damage support; `Laundry Barrage` adds a secondary squad damage multiplier.
- Evolution read: Laundry → Spin with stronger arm arcs → Royal with crown/bright terminal socks.
- Mutation hooks: mutation crown stays above head; charged traces can hop arm-to-arm.
- Animation opportunities: alternating arm punches, elastic sock-tip recoil, spin-cycle merge anticipation.
- IP-distance: generic octopus + socks/laundry, no branded pattern or copied cartoon octopus.

## Microwhale — unlock Chapter 24

**Visual joke:** a squat whale with a microwave-like belly chamber that charges an absurd snack-plasma beam.

- Silhouette primitive: very wide whale body + inset rectangular belly chamber.
- Dominant prop/material: teal rubbery skin, ceramic/glass chamber, warm plasma accents.
- Personality: slow, pleased heavy hitter; large body makes its slow cadence immediately legible.
- Gameplay role: late endless boss pressure; `Microwave Beam` adds a second boss-only damage multiplier.
- Evolution read: Snack → Plasma chamber with visible energy spokes → Supernova chamber/crown with the strongest warm core.
- Mutation hooks: overlays sit outside the belly window so the core remains readable.
- Animation opportunities: chamber glow charge, body compression before firing, hot-air wobble on release.
- IP-distance: generic whale + generic microwave visual vocabulary, no copied appliance faceplate/brand marks.

## Vacuumoon Overlord — endless boss

**Visual joke:** a moon-sized vacuum/turbine creature whose central suction disc behaves like a miniature gravity well.

- Silhouette primitive: massive rounded shell + dominant central circular turbine + antenna arms.
- Dominant material: cool enamel/metal with cyan, violet and pink rotor segments.
- Combat identity: relatively fast endless boss with a strong weak-point window; shield phase reads as `CYCLONE CHAMBER`, weak phase as `TURBINE HEART OPEN`.
- Telegraph: orbit pattern reinforces suction/rotation and remains shape-readable without color.
- Signature motion: rotor winds up opposite to idle before attack, then snaps forward.
- Defeat beat: inward implosion followed by loss of suction.
- IP-distance: generic vacuum/turbine/moon concept only; no commercial vacuum silhouette or logo.

## Blenderbehemoth Royale — endless boss

**Visual joke:** an enormous blender-beast whose blade cage becomes more dangerous as the encounter accelerates.

- Silhouette primitive: tall heavy vessel/body + large central blade cavity + blocky side handles.
- Dominant material: warm enamel, glass/ceramic cavity, high-contrast blade fan.
- Combat identity: heavier endless boss with high alpha damage and the strongest authored weak-window multiplier in the current roster.
- Telegraph: fan/cone telegraph communicates blade sweep direction without color dependence.
- Signature motion: body huff/pressure anticipation followed by blade snap.
- Defeat beat: violent spin-down/jam, not gore.
- IP-distance: original generic blender/appliance-monster design; no brand geometry, logos or copied character likeness.

## Balance guardrails

- New families unlock only after the initial three authored worlds are substantially learned; early Recruit readability remains intact.
- Secondary synergies stack multiplicatively at lower per-tier strength than their original counterpart to avoid instantly invalidating Core Lab progression.
- New endless bosses join the post-Chapter-15 rotation only; authored world-finale routing at Chapters 10 and 15 is unchanged.
- `Codex Complete` derives its target from the live roster length so future content waves do not silently leave a stale 21-form completion goal.
