# Brainrot Roster

Playable units are original Brainror Game IP. Internal family IDs remain stable for saves and gameplay systems, while public names and art follow the absurdist brainrot identity gate in `docs/BRAINROT_IDENTITY.md`. Every family has three merge tiers and a mundane food/object/vehicle fusion that must remain readable at board scale.

## Starter families — Chapter 1

### Pinguino

- Visual joke: penguin fused with a generic retro motor scooter and tiny espresso machinery.
- T1 **Motorino Pinguino** — simple scooter silhouette + one espresso cup.
- T2 **Espresso Pinguino** — larger coffee boiler, steam pipes and faster vehicle language.
- T3 **Grandissimo Pinguino** — oversized espresso engine, gold trim and grand-prix absurdity.
- Gameplay role: balanced cadence.
- Crew synergy: **Slipstream Relay** — squad attack haste.

### Toastodilo

- Visual joke: crocodile fused with focaccia/panzerotto and a countertop toaster-oven.
- T1 **Focaccino Crocodilo** — crust body, olive/tomato toppings, crocodile snout.
- T2 **Panzerotto Crocodilo** — stuffed pastry armor and visible cheese/oven mouth.
- T3 **Fornissimo Crocodilo** — giant bakery-oven body with a ridiculous heat crown.
- Gameplay role: heavy baseline damage / fortress support.
- Crew synergy: **Crust Bastion** — fortress damage resistance.

### Lampalotl

- Visual joke: axolotl fused with gelato/granita dessert hardware, explicitly replacing the old generic lava-lamp/alien read.
- T1 **Gelatino Axolotto** — axolotl scoop in a waffle cone.
- T2 **Granita Axolotto** — stacked icy dessert, straw/spoon accents and bigger gills.
- T3 **Gelatissimo Axolotto** — extravagant multi-scoop dessert silhouette with crown-wafer excess.
- Gameplay role: rapid-fire damage.
- Crew synergy: **Neon Cascade** — squad projectile damage.

### Dishnail

- Visual joke: snail fused with a metal colander and pasta artillery, replacing the old satellite-dish sci-fi read.
- T1 **Scolapasta Lumachino** — colander shell full of spaghetti.
- T2 **Raviolotto Lumachino** — ravioli armor + serving ladle.
- T3 **Pastissimo Lumachino** — absurd pasta cannon and overflowing colander shell.
- Gameplay role: artillery / alpha strike.
- Crew synergy: **Quasar Lock** — combat coin bounty.
