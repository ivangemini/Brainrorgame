# Store Creative Test Plan

## Goal
Choose the acquisition promise that produces the strongest qualified click-through without misrepresenting gameplay.

## Variant A — Chaos roster
Asset: `public/store/yandex-cover-chaos.svg`

Promise:
- absurd original creature roster;
- visible merge/collection fantasy;
- 36 forms and Endless Rift depth.

Primary hypothesis: younger casual players respond more strongly to the collectible character variety than to a single combat spectacle.

## Variant B — Boss combat
Asset: `public/store/yandex-cover-boss.svg`

Promise:
- readable player-vs-boss confrontation;
- synergies, mutations and Chaos Draft;
- immediate boss-rush identity.

Primary hypothesis: players who understand the game faster from the boss conflict produce higher install/start intent and stronger first-session completion.

## Measurement
For each portal that supports creative rotation, keep copy, category and targeting constant and vary only the hero creative. Track:
- impression -> game-page CTR;
- page -> game-start conversion where exposed;
- first meaningful interaction;
- first merge completion;
- first boss reach;
- 10-minute session survival;
- D1 return when the portal provides a stable anonymous cohort signal.

Do not select a winner on CTR alone. A creative that wins clicks but loses first-boss reach or early retention is attracting the wrong expectation.

## Decision rule
Use at least a meaningful traffic sample before promotion. Prefer the variant that improves qualified starts and first-boss reach without materially worsening D1. Keep both source variants because different portals may favor different promises.

## Creative safety
- Only project-owned original characters/bosses are shown.
- No borrowed meme characters, brands, celebrity likenesses or third-party marks.
- Numbers used in creatives must match the current production roster.
- Do not show mechanics that are not available in the submitted build.
